NFL DFS Lab performance/data fix

1) nflverse PBP
- Week 1 of a new season now uses prior-season weeks 14-18 as its baseline.
- If current-season PBP isn't published yet, the endpoint automatically falls back one season.
- Parsed compact PBP is cached in Render memory for 6 hours.
- The correct nflverse release URL remains:
  /releases/download/pbp/play_by_play_YEAR.csv.gz

2) Optimizer
- Default attempts reduced from 30,000 to 2,500.
- Hard maximum reduced to 5,000.
- Candidate lists are precomputed once and trimmed to the best 30-42 players per slot.
- Early-stop once enough distinct candidate lineups are found.
- This removes repeated full player-pool filtering and is intended for iPhone/mobile use.

Expected result:
- Generate 20 lineups in seconds rather than timing out.
- 2026 Week 1 red-zone load should say it is using a 2025 baseline rather than returning 404.
